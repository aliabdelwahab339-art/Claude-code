# $100M Leads by Alex Hormozi

## Core Idea
Leads are the lifeblood of any business, and the best business skill you can have is the ability to make strangers aware of your offer. This book presents the "Core Four" advertising methods — warm outreach, free content, cold outreach, and paid ads — and shows how to combine them to generate unlimited leads. The business that gets the most leads wins.

## Key Frameworks

### The Leads → Engaged Leads → Customers Funnel
- **Lead**: someone who might buy your stuff
- **Engaged Lead**: someone who has shown interest (replied, clicked, opted in)
- **Buyer**: someone who has paid

The goal of advertising is to move strangers from unaware → engaged → buying.

### The Core Four Advertising Methods
1. **Warm Outreach** — Reach out to people who already know you (friends, past customers, network). Highest conversion rate. Message: "I have something new, thought of you."
2. **Post Free Content** — Publish valuable content on any platform to grow a warm audience. The content is not the asset — the audience is.
3. **Cold Outreach** — Contact strangers via DM, email, cold call. Lower conversion but unlimited scale.
4. **Run Paid Ads** — Pay platforms (Meta, Google, YouTube) to put your offer in front of targeted strangers.

### The Lead Magnet
A free, highly specific item of value that solves one problem for your ideal customer. Purpose: get strangers to raise their hand and give you permission to follow up.
- Must be a "complete solution" to a narrow problem
- Creates desire for the paid offer by solving the entry problem

### Core Four on Steroids: Lead Getters
Four ways to get other people to generate leads for you:
1. **Customer Referrals** — Ask happy customers to refer others. The simplest, cheapest lead source.
2. **Employees** — Sales team doing outreach on your behalf
3. **Agencies** — Pay someone to run ads or outreach
4. **Affiliates & Partners** — Revenue share with people who promote your offer

### The Content Unit Framework
All content has three parts:
- **Hook**: Get them to stop and pay attention
- **Retain**: Keep them engaged through the body
- **Reward**: Deliver value so they come back

Content types: Far Past stories, Recent Past stories, Present updates, Trending topics, and Manufactured content.

### The 9-Word Email (Dean Jackson)
To re-engage cold or dormant leads:
> "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?"

No images, no links, no frills. Just a question. Gets replies from leads who are ready to buy.

### Advertising Math Benchmark
- Warm reach outs → ~20% engage
- Of those who engage → ~20% take free offer
- Of those who take free offer → ~25% convert to paid
- Result: ~1 customer per 100 warm reach outs

### Open-to-Goal Framework
Work backwards from your revenue goal:
1. What revenue do I need?
2. How many customers is that?
3. How many leads do I need to hit that customer number?
4. Which advertising method gets me those leads?

## Actionable Takeaways
- Start with warm outreach — highest ROI, zero ad spend required
- Your audience is the compounding asset, not the content itself
- A lead magnet should solve one specific problem so well it creates desire for more
- Post content 10x more than you think you should — volume beats cleverness
- Use the "9-word email" to resurrect dead leads instantly
- Don't diversify advertising methods until you've maxed out one channel
- Referrals are the cheapest leads — build a referral ask into your fulfillment process
- Use the Open-to-Goal formula to reverse-engineer your daily lead target
- $250M+ annual revenue portfolio = 20,000+ leads per day; it scales with this system
- "It's hard to be poor with leads banging down your door"
