# طورشيكو — Brand Brief

## What the Company Does
طورشيكو is an Egyptian pickles brand launching from scratch with a mission to dominate the Egyptian pickles market through a unique, proprietary recipe and unforgettable flavor profile. The brand targets both individual consumers (B2C) and restaurants/food businesses (B2B) across Egypt.

## Products
- Full range of Egyptian pickles: طرشي مشكل, ليمون, زيتون, فلفل, جزر, خيار, لفت, باذنجان, and more
- Core differentiator: خلطة سرية وطعم فريد — a secret recipe producing a taste unlike anything on the market

## Stage
Startup — Pre-launch. Zero traction yet. Building brand identity and awareness from day one.

## Market
- Geography: Egypt (primary)
- Channel: TBD (retail, online, direct to restaurants)

## Target Customers
1. **Individuals / Families** — Egyptians who love traditional pickles at home
2. **Restaurants & Food Businesses** — Need consistent, quality pickles as a staple side dish

## Business Goals
- Create massive brand awareness on launch ("حاجه تكسر الدنيا")
- Establish طورشيكو as the #1 premium pickles brand in Egypt
- Drive trial purchases (first taste = loyal customer)
- Land restaurant accounts for B2B revenue stream

## Revenue Model
- Direct-to-consumer (jars sold online + retail)
- Wholesale to restaurants and catering businesses

## Founder Story / Origin
New Egyptian brand. Proprietary recipe. Built to disrupt a market dominated by generic, commodity pickles. The founders believe Egyptians deserve better — a pickle with personality, flavor, and pride.

## Why Now
The Egyptian food market is shifting toward premium, artisanal products. Social media–driven food brands are exploding. طورشيكو is positioned to ride this wave as the first "personality-forward" pickles brand in Egypt.
