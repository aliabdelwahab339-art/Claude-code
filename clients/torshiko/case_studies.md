# طورشيكو — Case Studies & Proof Points

## Status
Brand is pre-launch — no customer case studies yet.

## Planned Proof Strategy

### Phase 1: Seeding
- Send free jars to 20–30 food influencers and food bloggers in Egypt
- Document their reactions (video unboxings, taste tests)
- Collect quotes and testimonials before public launch

### Phase 2: Restaurant Pilots
- Offer 5–10 restaurants a free trial supply for 2 weeks
- Collect feedback: customer reactions, reorder intent
- Use restaurant owner testimonials as B2B social proof

### Phase 3: Launch Social Proof
- First 100 customers get discount + asked to share their reaction
- UGC (user-generated content) strategy: repost customer reactions
- Build "wall of love" on website/Instagram

---

## Reference Success Stories (Comparable Brands for Inspiration)

### El-Hawag (الحواج) — Egypt
Became a beloved Egyptian food brand through quality consistency and strong offline word-of-mouth. Lesson: taste + reliability = loyal customers.

### Bazooka / Juhayna premium lines
Premium positioning in commodity categories works in Egypt when backed by distinctive branding and marketing.

### Regional: Artisanal pickle brands in Lebanon/Jordan
Small artisanal brands built loyal followings through "made with love" narratives and beautiful packaging — then scaled.
