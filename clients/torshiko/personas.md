# طورشيكو — Ideal Customer Profiles

---

## Persona 1: فريدة — ست البيت المصرية
**Age:** 28–50
**Location:** Cairo, Alexandria, Delta cities
**Role:** Homemaker or working woman who manages household food

### Demographics
- Married, 1–3 kids
- Middle to upper-middle class
- Shops at Carrefour, Hyper One, or orders online

### Psychographics
- Values authentic Egyptian taste — "زي ما مامتي كانت بتعمل"
- Proud of serving good food to her family
- Skeptical of generic supermarket brands
- Trusts word-of-mouth and social proof

### Pain Profile
- Most pickles in the market taste the same — bland, too sour, too salty
- Can't always make homemade pickles (time, effort)
- Wants something that feels homemade but is convenient

### Buying Triggers
- Sees viral video or reel about طورشيكو
- Friend recommends it
- Sees it at supermarket and recognizes the brand from social media
- Price is fair for the quality perceived

### Language They Use
- "عايزة طعم حقيقي مش طعم مصنع"
- "المخلل ده ببقى جزء أساسي في كل أكلة"
- "لقيت حاجة بطعم البيت!"

---

## Persona 2: محمود — صاحب مطعم
**Age:** 30–55
**Location:** Cairo, Giza, Alexandria
**Role:** Restaurant owner or purchasing manager

### Demographics
- Runs a local Egyptian restaurant, koshary shop, grills, or catering business
- Buys in bulk, cares about consistency and price
- Makes decisions based on customer reaction

### Psychographics
- Customers always ask for "the good pickle"
- Wants to differentiate his restaurant
- Values reliable supply and fair wholesale pricing

### Pain Profile
- Generic pickles hurt his restaurant's reputation
- Inconsistent quality from current suppliers
- Customers notice when pickles are bad — and comment about it

### Buying Triggers
- Tastes طورشيكو and it's clearly superior
- Sales rep offers a free sample or trial order
- Sees other restaurants using it
- Wholesale pricing makes business sense

### Language They Use
- "اللي بيميز المطعم هو التفاصيل الصغيرة"
- "الزبون بيلاحظ المخلل"
- "عايز مورد بيوفر وجودة ثابتة"

---

## Persona 3: أحمد — الشاب الفودي
**Age:** 20–32
**Location:** Cairo, urban areas
**Social media:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook

### Demographics
- Works or studies, disposable income
- Follows food accounts, tries new things
- Orders food online frequently

### Psychographics
- FOMO-driven — tries anything that goes viral
- Shares food experiences on social media
- Proud to discover new Egyptian brands before his friends

### Buying Triggers
- Sees a viral TikTok/Reel about طورشيكو
- Influencer he follows tries it
- Distinctive packaging catches his eye
- Brand has personality and humor

### Language They Use
- "ده الكلام يا صاحبي"
- "لازم تجرب ده"
- "المخلل ده على مستوى تاني خالص"
