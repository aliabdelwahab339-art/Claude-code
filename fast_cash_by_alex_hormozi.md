# Fast Cash by Alex Hormozi
## ($100M Playbook: Fast Cash — The Fastest Way to Rip Cash Out of a Business)

## Core Idea
Fast Cash Plays are limited-time, high-ticket offers sold to your warmest audiences (existing customers, previous customers, engaged leads). Run them 4 times per year. They generate large cash spikes with zero acquisition cost because you already paid to get these people. In the early days of Gym Launch, Fast Cash Plays were only ~15% of annual revenue but nearly **50% of profit**. This single playbook can buy you a nice car in a week or two from a few emails and texts.

## Key Frameworks

### What Fast Cash Plays Are
**Fast Cash Plays = limited-time, high-ticket offers sold to warm audiences.**

They are NOT:
- Normal lead nurture to new prospects
- Long-term content emails to keep lists warm
- Your main ongoing offer

They ARE:
- High-ticket (10x-50x your normal offer price)
- Limited spots (scarcity)
- Exclusive (for existing customers only)
- Time-bounded (deadline creates urgency)
- Run quarterly (every ~90 days)

### The 10x the 10% Rule
**If you get 10% of your customers to pay 10x the price, you double your revenue.**

- 1 in 9 Americans is a millionaire — the big spenders in your audience don't advertise themselves
- People who want to spend more money on you can't if you don't give them something to buy
- A Fast Cash Play unlocks spending from your highest-value customers
- All extra revenue (minus delivery) drops straight to bottom line — no acquisition cost

Example math:
- 100 customers × $100/month normal = $10K/month
- 10 customers × $1,000 one-time Fast Cash offer = $10K bonus cash
- Revenue doubles; the $10K costs nearly nothing to generate

### Who to Advertise To
Fast Cash Plays target three groups (in priority order):
1. **Existing customers** — highest trust, highest willingness to buy, most familiar with your value
2. **Previous customers** — already experienced your product; win-back opportunity
3. **Engaged leads** — gave you permission to contact them; haven't bought yet but have expressed interest

These audiences are free to reach (email, text, internal community) — zero ad spend required.

### What to Sell
Fast Cash offers should be:
- **Unscalable** — something you can only offer to a small number of people (limits supply → creates scarcity)
- **High-touch** — direct access to you or your best people
- **Your most valuable offer** — the thing you'd charge the most for because it requires the most of you
- **Close to the core** — solves the same problem as your main offer, just at the highest possible level

Examples across industries:
- **Gym:** VIP personal training package (10 sessions with the head coach) at $5,000+
- **Marketing agency:** Done-for-you campaign sprint at 10x monthly retainer
- **Coaching/consulting:** A day of direct access to the founder at $10,000-$50,000
- **Software/SaaS:** White-glove implementation with personal onboarding at 5x normal price

### How to Price It
- Price at 10x-50x your normal ticket
- Use limited spots (5-10 buyers max per round) to signal exclusivity and create scarcity
- Price high enough that only 5-10% of your list will buy — that's the goal, not everyone
- "Price like you mean it" — if you're not nervous about the price, it's not high enough

### The 7-Day Campaign Sequence
Run the promotion over 7 days:

**Day 1:** Announcement email/text — what it is, why it's rare, limited spots
**Day 2:** Value email — share a success story or case study of what's possible
**Day 3:** Objection-handling email — address the most common hesitation
**Day 4:** Reminder email — "spots filling up"
**Day 5:** Urgency email — "only X spots left"
**Day 6:** Final warning — "last chance, closing tomorrow"
**Day 7:** Last call — closes at midnight

Two delivery paths:
- **Push to Consult** — drives to a sales call (higher ticket, more persuasion needed)
- **Push to Automated Checkout** — drives directly to a payment page (lower ticket, impulse buy)

### Fast Cash Play Outline
1. Decide who you're advertising to (existing customers first)
2. Pick your offer (most valuable, unscalable thing you can deliver)
3. Set price (10x-50x normal ticket)
4. Cap spots (5-10 maximum)
5. Set a hard deadline (7 days or less)
6. Run the 7-day email/text sequence
7. Either push to consult (phone) or push to automated checkout

### 5 Reasons to Run Fast Cash Offers Every 90 Days
1. **Cash emergencies** — you'll always have unexpected expenses; this is your get-out-of-jail-free card
2. **Tax bills, equipment, legal fees** — real-world example that triggered the original Fast Cash Play for Hormozi
3. **Profit maximization** — the incremental cost to deliver Fast Cash offer is near zero; nearly all revenue is profit
4. **List activation** — re-engages dormant customers who've been meaning to buy more
5. **LTV increase** — each Fast Cash round increases LTGP of your customer base

### ROI Benchmarks (Real Gym Launch Data)
- Average business: +$30,000 cash from one Fast Cash Play
- Businesses that priced at $5,000+: $50,000+ per play
- Hormozi promise: "If you execute only this playbook, you will more than ROI any investment you put into it."
