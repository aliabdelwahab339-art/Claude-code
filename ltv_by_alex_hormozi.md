# Lifetime Value (LTV) by Alex Hormozi
## ($100M Playbook: Lifetime Value — Eight Ways to Get Customers to Buy More Stuff)

## Core Idea
LTV (Lifetime Value) is the fuel of advertising. The business owner who makes a customer more valuable than the competition can outspend them on every advertising platform — and win. There are exactly eight ways to increase LTV, called The Crazy Eight. Most businesses only use one or two. Unlocking even two or three more levers can 2x-5x revenue without acquiring a single new customer.

## Key Frameworks

### What Is LTV (LTGP)?
**LTV = Lifetime Gross Profit collected over the lifespan of a customer**

Also called Lifetime Gross Profit (LTGP) or Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) — same concept.

Not revenue — gross profit. Gross profit is what's left after delivering the product/service, before overhead.

### How to Calculate LTV

**Step 1: Calculate Gross Profit**
- Product: Sale price minus cost to manufacture and deliver
- Example: Widget at $100, costs $20 to make/ship → Gross Profit = $80

- Service: Revenue per client minus cost to deliver
- Example: $3,000/month client, one rep handles 10 clients at $6,000/month cost → $24,000 gross profit per rep / 10 clients = $2,400 gross profit per client per month

**Step 2: Figure Out Average Transactions OR Churn**
- For transactional businesses: export lifetime customer data → average transactions per customer
- For recurring businesses: calculate monthly churn rate

Churn formula:
- Last period: 100 customers
- This period: 95 customers
- Churn = (100-95) / 100 = 5%

**Step 3: Calculate LTGP**
- Transactional: Gross Profit × Average Transactions = LTGP
  - Example: $80 × 4 transactions = $320 LTGP
- Recurring: Gross Profit ÷ Churn % = LTGP
  - Example: $2,400 / 5% = $48,000 LTGP

### Why LTV Is the Winning Lever
- Ad costs only go up over time (more competition for attention)
- The only way to win an advertising auction long-term: make your customer worth more than competitors can
- If your LTGP is $300 and your competitor's is $100, you can outbid them 3:1 for the same customer
- This creates what Hormozi calls a "veritable monopoly of attention" — no one can outbid you

### The Crazy Eight — All 8 Ways to Increase LTV

**Increase Revenue Side:**
1. **Increase Prices** — Raise the price of your existing offer. Works when perceived value exceeds current price. The simplest LTV lever. Most businesses undercharge.

2. **Increase # of Purchases (Frequency)** — Get customers to buy more often. Triggers: habit formation, reminders, re-engagement sequences, subscription models, auto-refill.

3. **Sell More (Increase Quantity)** — Sell larger quantities per transaction. Bundles, multi-packs, bulk pricing. Less convenience friction per unit = more units sold.

4. **Sell Better (Upgrade Quality)** — Upsell customers to a higher tier, premium version, or VIP experience. The key: the new offer solves the same problem but with faster results, less effort, less risk, and more support — for more money.

5. **Cross-Sell Something Different** — Sell something adjacent that solves the customer's next problem. Key rule: it must be close to the core product. Customers who liked Product A want Product B if it's a natural next step — not if it's an entirely different category.

**Decrease Cost Side:**
6. **Decrease Costs** — Reduce the cost to deliver the product/service without reducing perceived value. Scale efficiencies, better vendors, automation, process improvements.

**Downsell Side (Capture Near-Churners):**
7. **Downsell Fewer (Lower Quantity)** — When a customer is about to cancel or downgrade, offer a smaller version instead of losing them entirely. A smaller yes is better than a no.

8. **Downsell Lower Quality** — Offer a stripped-down, lower-cost tier for customers who can't afford the full version. Keeps them in the ecosystem.

### The Best Upsell Formula
"More of — or more help with — what they just bought, but with faster results, less risk, less effort, less hassle — for more money."

This upsell formula: 2.2x LTV in one real case study. Enabled 5x scaling of advertising spend profitably.

### The Upsell Survey Principle
When you don't know what to upsell: **ask customers**. Don't create what you're passionate about — create what they want next. The business serves the customer, not the other way around.

### LTV Math Impact
Adding a backend product that 1 in 5 customers buys can double revenue:
- 1,000 customers × $100/month = $100K/month
- 200 customers (20%) buy $500 upsell = $100K additional
- Revenue doubles with no new advertising cost
