## Core Principles

### 1. Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
Customers don't buy products — they hire them to make progress in their lives.
- Always identify the functional, emotional, and social jobs the product fulfills
- Messaging should speak to the progress, not the product
- "People don't want a quarter-inch drill, they want a quarter-inch hole"

### 2. The Hook → Value → CTA Framework
Every piece of content must follow this structure:
- **Hook**: Stop the scroll in the first 2 seconds / first line
- **Value**: Deliver a real insight, transformation, or emotion
- **CTA**: One clear, low-friction next step

### 3. Specificity Over Generality
Specific claims build trust. Vague claims erode it.
- Bad: "We help businesses grow"
- Good: "Helped 47 SaaS founders go from $0 to $10K MRR in 90 days"
- Use numbers, timeframes, and named outcomes wherever possible

### 4. The Differentiation Ladder
Before writing a single word, climb this ladder:
1. What do competitors say? → Never say that
2. What does the market believe? → Challenge or confirm it
3. What does our brand uniquely believe? → Lead with that

### 5. Emotion First, Logic Second
People decide with emotion and justify with logic.
- Lead with emotion (fear, aspiration, frustration, relief)
- Follow with features, proof, and logic
- Close with identity ("This is who you are when you use X")

### 6. The 1-3-1 Content Rule
- 1 idea per piece of content
- 3 supporting points or proof elements
- 1 clear takeaway or action

### 7. Platform-Native Thinking
Content that works on LinkedIn fails on TikTok. Never repurpose without reformatting.
- Instagram: Visual-first, hook in first frame, community-driven
- TikTok: Raw, authentic, trend-aware, fast-paced
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership, professional transformation stories
- YouTube: Long-form value, searchable titles, strong thumbnails
- Facebook: Community, offers, social proof
