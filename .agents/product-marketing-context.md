# Product Marketing Context

This file provides foundational product marketing context used across all agency campaigns.
It is read by agents to ground their work in proven positioning and messaging principles.

---

## Agency Positioning

This is an AI-native marketing agency. Our campaigns are powered by:
- Deep competitor intelligence (not assumptions)
- Creator and content research (validated hooks, not guesses)
- Multi-agent parallel execution (speed without sacrificing quality)
- Personal frameworks compiled from the owner's mental models

## What We Sell (for our clients)

We don't sell features. We sell:
- **Transformation**: What the customer's life looks like after
- **Relief**: The pain they escape from
- **Identity**: The person they become by using the product
- **Proof**: Evidence that the transformation is real and achievable

## Campaign Quality Standards

Every campaign deliverable must be:
1. **Specific** — real numbers, real outcomes, real timelines
2. **Differentiated** — zero competitor clichés
3. **Platform-native** — formatted for how that platform is consumed
4. **Action-oriented** — every CTA has one clear next step
5. **Brand-consistent** — voice, tone, and values match the guidelines

## Research Philosophy

Good marketing starts with intelligence, not opinion:
- Research competitors before writing a single word
- Identify what the top creators in the niche are doing
- Find the content that breaks through the algorithm (viral threshold data)
- Map competitor ad strategies before running ads

## Output Philosophy

Quantity without quality is waste. We produce:
- 30 social posts that are genuinely scroll-stopping
- 10 Google ads with real keyword strategy behind them
- 10 Meta ads informed by competitor ad library analysis
- Copy that a real human would read and act on
