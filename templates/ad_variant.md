## 10-Variant Angle Matrix

Ensure each campaign tests these 10 angles across variants:

| # | Angle | Core message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pain agitation | "Tired of [frustration]?" |
| 2 | Aspiration | "Imagine [desired outcome]" |
| 3 | Social proof | "[N] people / companies / results" |
| 4 | Fear of missing out | "While you're [status quo], competitors are..." |
| 5 | Curiosity / open loop | "The [unexpected thing] that [result]" |
| 6 | Contrarian / bold claim | "Stop doing [conventional thing]" |
| 7 | How-to / education | "How [persona] [achieved result] without [obstacle]" |
| 8 | Comparison | "[Us] vs. [category norm] — here's the difference" |
| 9 | Objection demolisher | "Yes, even if [common objection]" |
| 10 | Story / transformation | "I was [before]. Now [after]. Here's how." |
