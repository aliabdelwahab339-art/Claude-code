# Social Post Template

Use this format when generating platform-specific social posts.
Each post must follow the Hook → Value → CTA structure.

---

## Instagram Post

**Format:** [Carousel / Single image / Reel / Story]
**Hook (first line — must stop the scroll):**
[Hook text — max 125 characters before "more" cut-off]

**Body:**
[3–7 short paragraphs or bullets. Each paragraph = 1–2 lines max.
Use line breaks for readability. No walls of text.]

**CTA:**
[One clear action: "Save this", "Drop a comment", "Link in bio", "DM me X"]

**Hashtags:**
[10–15 relevant hashtags — mix of niche, community, and broad]

**Alt text for image:**
[Describe the visual for accessibility]

---

## TikTok / Reel Script

**Hook (0–3 seconds):**
[What's said / shown on screen to grab attention immediately]

**Problem agitation (3–15 seconds):**
[Identify the pain or situation]

**Payoff / value (15–45 seconds):**
[The insight, tip, transformation, or story]

**CTA (last 3–5 seconds):**
[Follow, comment, check link in bio]

**On-screen text:** [Key words to flash on screen]
**Music/audio direction:** [Trending audio type or vibe]

---

## LinkedIn Post

**Hook (first line — appears before "see more"):**
[Bold statement, counterintuitive claim, or compelling question]

**Story / insight:**
[Personal anecdote or business lesson — 150–300 words]
[Use single-sentence paragraphs and white space]

**Takeaway:**
[1–3 bullet points or numbered list]

**CTA:**
[Engage the audience: question, poll, "save this", "share with someone who needs it"]

[Question or statement that prompts engagement]

**Body:**
[Context or value — 2–4 sentences]

## Facebook Post

[Relatable statement or question directed at the audience segment]

[Longer form than IG — can be 200–400 words for FB]
