# $100M Money Models by Alex Hormozi

## Core Idea
A Money Model is the architecture of how your business makes money from each customer — not just the first sale, but every transaction across the relationship. By engineering the right sequence of offers (attraction → upsell → downsell → continuity), businesses can make so much money in the first 30 days that customer acquisition costs become irrelevant.

## Key Frameworks

### The Four Types of Offers in a Money Model
Every business needs offers in four categories:

1. **Attraction Offers** — Get new people in the door; often free or deeply discounted. Purpose: generate flow.
2. **Upsell Offers** — Sell more to someone who just bought. The highest-converting moment is immediately after a purchase.
3. **Downsell Offers** — Capture buyers who declined the upsell with a smaller, alternative version.
4. **Continuity Offers** — Recurring revenue. The compounding asset of any business.

### Attraction Offers (Section II)
Ways to get new customers:
- **Win Your Money Back** — lottery-style promotion where buyers can win their purchase price back
- **Giveaways** — free items to build list and awareness
- **Decoy Offer** — an intentionally inferior option that makes the real offer look better by comparison
- **Buy X Get Y Free** — perceived value bundle
- **Pay Less Now or Pay More Later** — trial pricing with price lock
- **Free Goodwill Offer** — give genuine value with no strings attached to build trust

### Upsell Offers (Section III)
The highest-leverage moment in any sales funnel:
- **Classic Upsell** — "Would you like X for $Y more?"
- **Menu Upsell** — present 3 options at point of purchase; middle option gets the most clicks
- **Anchor Upsell** — show a premium option first so the real upsell feels cheap by comparison
- **Rollover Upsell** — automatically add a recurring component at checkout

### Downsell Offers (Section IV)
Don't let "no" be the final answer:
- **Payment Plan Downsell** — same offer, spread over time
- **Trial With Penalty** — low entry price but penalties for leaving early
- **Feature Downsell** — stripped-down version of the main offer

### Continuity Offers (Section V)
The ultimate business asset — recurring revenue that compounds:
- **Continuity Bonus** — add a subscription as a bonus to a one-time offer
- **Continuity Discount** — discount the subscription vs. buying month-by-month
- **Waived Fee Offer** — waive a recurring fee as long as the customer stays active

### The Storage Unit Story (Free Offer Lesson)
Hormozi's mentor showed him: a "free month of storage" actually generates $127 in revenue through a lock ($47) + boxes + insurance + upsizes. The lesson: make the free offer truly free, but engineer the natural sequence of purchases that follow.

### The "Ten Years in Ten Minutes" Model
When entering a new business or market, Hormozi's shortcut:
1. Look at the most successful competitors
2. Map every offer they make from first contact to backend
3. Reverse-engineer their entire money model
4. Launch with a proven model, not an untested one

### The $100M Money Model Goal
Engineer offers such that: **the cost to acquire a customer is recovered within the first 30 days**, so the entire business runs on profit from day one. This eliminates the need for large capital to fund growth.

## Actionable Takeaways
- Map your current money model — how many of the 4 offer types do you use? Add the missing ones.
- Add a continuity component to every non-recurring offer immediately
- Engineer your attraction offer to make the natural next purchase obvious (like the free storage unit)
- Always have a upsell ready at the point of purchase — this is your highest-converting moment
- Use the Menu Upsell (3 options at checkout) — the middle option consistently outperforms
- When studying competitors, map their full money model, not just their front-end offer
- The goal: recover CAC within 30 days so all revenue beyond that is pure profit
- A Payment Plan downsell recovers up to 30% of "no" responses
- Free Goodwill offers build trust faster than any paid promotion
- "The business that can make the most money from a customer wins" — engineer the full journey
